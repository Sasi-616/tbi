Programs & Thrust Verticals

Incubation Tracks Tailored for Deep Engineering

From early concept ideation to high-throughput prototyping and multi-crore market commercialization.

Vertical 01

Agriculture & Food Processing Technology

Located in Guntur, Asia’s largest chilli market and a global spice hub. We support hardware sorters, non-destructive moisture testers, solar dehydrators, drone imaging, and bio-formulations.

Dedicated Laboratory Infrastructure:

• Food Testing Suite & HPLC Analytical Chemistry Lab

• Post-Harvest Solar Drying Testbed

• Farm Sensor Calibration & Drone Payload Prototyping Station

Vertical 02

Biotechnology & Molecular Healthcare

Translational genomics, point-of-care diagnostics, and indigenous reagents. Birthplace of Shubhakruth Medical Genetics Pvt. Ltd. (SMGPL), accelerated by VFSTR TBI Council and now supplying clinical NGS and test kits pan-India.

Dedicated Laboratory Infrastructure:

• Class 10,000 Molecular Biology Cleanrooms

• Real-Time PCR (Bio-Rad CFX96) & Qubit 4 Fluorometers

• -80°C Ultra-Low Freezers & Cold-Chain Logistics Hub

Vertical 03

Renewable Energy & Power Systems

Hybrid solar-biomass systems, decentralized cold rooms for agricultural produce, micro-inverters, and battery testing jigs.

Dedicated Laboratory Infrastructure:

• Centre of Excellence in Renewable Energy

• Solar PV Array Emulators & Grid-Tie Inverters

• Biomass Gasifier Characterization Rig

Vertical 04

Waste Valorization & Circular Tech

Transforming agricultural crop residues into bio-char, value-added industrial enzymes, wastewater reclamation, and sustainable bio-composites.

Dedicated Laboratory Infrastructure:

• Environmental Engineering & Effluent Characterization Lab

• Thermal Pyrolysis & Bio-Char Development Reactor

• Material Tensile Testing & Composite Molding Station
