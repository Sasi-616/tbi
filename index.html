VFSTR TBI Council is a Section 8 non-profit company (CIN: U73100AP2022NPL121922) operating from the 2nd Floor of Nagarjuna Bhavan at Vignan’s Foundation for Science, Technology and Research (Deemed to be University).

Surrounded by 4 Centres of Excellence and 15 advanced research laboratories, Vignan TBI links regional industrial challenges in chilli, spices, biotech, and renewable power with top researchers and entrepreneurial grit. In 2023, the Department of Science & Technology (DST) sanctioned ₹4.50 Crore to accelerate this ecosystem as a full-fledged NIDHI inclusive TBI.

Everything You Need to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, transparent answers about eligibility, seed funding, lab access, and IP protection.

Who is eligible to apply for incubation at Vignan TBI?
Incubation is open to students, researchers, faculty innovators, alumni of VFSTR, as well as external grassroots entrepreneurs and startups across Andhra Pradesh and pan-India. If your venture works in Agritech, Biotechnology, Clean Energy, Waste Management, or DeepTech, you can apply directly.
What funding support is available through DST NIDHI?
Through the ₹4.50 Crore DST NIDHI inclusive TBI grant, Vignan TBI deploys several mechanisms: NIDHI PRAYAS prototyping grants (up to ₹10 Lakhs non-dilutive), NIDHI EIR monthly entrepreneur stipends (up to ₹30,000/month for 12 months), and Seed Support System (SSS) tranches up to ₹50 Lakhs for growth-stage entities. Over ₹60 Lakhs in seed has already been deployed.
Can our team access university research laboratories and test equipment?
Yes. Incubated companies receive designated access badges to the 4 Centres of Excellence and 15 research centers housed at Nagarjuna Bhavan. This includes biotechnology cleanrooms, spectrophotometry, real-time PCR, solar simulators, high-speed hardware 3D printers, and chemical characterization benches.
Does Vignan TBI claim ownership over our startup’s intellectual property (IP)?
No. The intellectual property generated by the founders remains 100% with the founder and their registered company. Vignan TBI provides institutional IP attorneys to assist with patent search, drafting, and fast-track filing, without claiming IP ownership.
How long does the application review and screening process take?
Initial review of submitted applications takes 7 to 10 working days. Shortlisted founders are invited for an IdeaIgnite pitch session before our screening panel. Once cleared, pre-incubation or full incubation onboarding occurs immediately.
