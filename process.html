Founder Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about residency, grants, and legal intellectual property.

Do I need to be a student or faculty of Vignan to apply?

No. Vignan TBI is a DST NIDHI inclusive TBI. While we actively incubate student and faculty innovations, our doors are open to external founders, independent researchers, and startups from across India.

Who owns the Intellectual Property (IP) developed during incubation?

Founders retain 100% ownership of their inventions and patents. Vignan TBI provides in-house patent attorneys and financial subsidies to expedite your filings without claiming background or foreground IP.

Is physical presence required in Vadlamudi, Guntur?

We offer both Resident Incubation (recommended for wet-lab biotech, food processing, and IoT hardware projects requiring specialized campus equipment) and Virtual / Hybrid Incubation for digital platforms.

What happens after the 18–24 month incubation period?

Startups graduate upon achieving commercial revenue or closing institutional venture capital rounds. Graduated companies join our Alumni Founder Syndicate and maintain ongoing subsidized access to our research facilities.
